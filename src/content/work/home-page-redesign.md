## Context

<p class="lede">As a new e-commerce startup, we were struggling to convert early mobile traffic due to a high bounce rate, and our homepage failed to clearly communicate value or build trust.</p>

### What's the problem? What are the goals?

<div class="cards">
  <div><h5>📈 Business Needs</h5><p>Validate product-market fit quickly by increasing the conversion rate and building customer trust.</p></div>
  <div><h5>❓ Problem to Solve</h5><p>Users exited our homepage within seconds due to a poor mobile experience, scan­ability challenges, and lack of trust.</p></div>
  <div><h5>📍 Project Goal</h5><p>Redesign the homepage with a mobile-first approach that improves clarity, reduces friction, and increases conversion rate.</p></div>
  <div><h5>🪨 Challenge</h5><p>How can we quickly deliver a homepage that balances brand storytelling and usability, while working within engineering constraints and aligning with both user behavior and marketing priorities?</p></div>
</div>

### Solutions

I redesigned our home page with a mobile-first approach focused on clarity, trust, and reducing friction.

<div class="cards three">
  <div><h5>Mobile-First Experience</h5><ul><li>Make hero banners swipeable</li><li>Add “View All” category shortcuts</li><li>Remove branding block to prioritize product content</li></ul></div>
  <div><h5>Improve Scannability</h5><ul><li>Shorten collection titles and subtitles</li><li>Add collection list components for easier browsing</li></ul></div>
  <div><h5>Build Trust</h5><ul><li>Enhance credibility with a clean, pixel-perfect homepage design</li><li>Introduce an about page</li></ul></div>
</div>

## Process

### Research Root Causes of Low Conversion

I conducted a UX audit, Hotjar analysis, and GA4 review to uncover why users dropped off our website and failed to convert.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Db6JWBKaQ5nAJmZsmiNOobDzs.png?width=2373&height=1377)

### Prioritized high-impact, high-urgency projects

I chose to redesign the homepage first based on effort, design impact, and urgency to boost conversion rates.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/7wpAqoVtcTp4nxf0awh1kraUHcI.png?width=2373&height=1377)

### Users Dropped Off Due to Poor Mobile Usability

Session replays and analytics revealed that users quickly scrolled past the banner and exited. Key CTAs were buried below the fold, making it hard to navigate. Combined with cluttered content and inconsistent layout hierarchy, the homepage failed to deliver clear value, hurting both scan­ability and user trust.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/HCPHbrlVk2CeqcfbUb4fv6ngg.png?width=2373&height=1377)

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/FVFJkZAK2qxoaiqqS3hhfRrCcmo.png?width=2373&height=1377)

### Prototyping & Feedback

I created mid- to high-fidelity prototypes in Figma, then presented to marketing, engineering, and product teams.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/g9AW2gVZr5YwsNjG8OTOCipkgvc.png?width=2373&height=1377)

### Front-End Deployment with AI Agent

I leveraged Shopify’s AI agent to accelerate the development, testing, and successful deployment of the storefront's front-end code.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/64MWQiWislscrNZ1G7J4QepqOaM.png?width=2373&height=1377)

#### Users quickly discover key deals and categories above the fold, reducing friction and increasing engagement.

<div class="cards three">
  <div><h5>PROBLEM SOLVED</h5><p>Previously, users scrolled past the banner without engaging, missed key categories hidden below the fold, and got overwhelmed by cluttered messaging and layout confusion.</p></div>
  <div><h5>HOW</h5><p>The redesigned layout introduces touch-friendly category shortcuts placed above the fold. The promotional banner is shortened and visually balanced, creating clear visual hierarchy between content and CTAs.</p></div>
  <div><h5>WHY</h5><p>By applying the principle of clarity and accessibility first, the new homepage helps users understand value instantly and encourages interaction without cognitive effort.</p></div>

## Impact

  <div><b class="up" data-count="37" data-suffix="%">37%</b><span>Home Page Session Duration</span></div>
  <div><b class="up" data-count="26" data-suffix="%">26%</b><span>Add-to-Cart Rate</span></div>
  <div><b class="up" data-count="49" data-suffix="%">49%</b><span>Checkout Reach Rate</span></div>
</div>

### What I Learned From Unexpected Data Results & Next Steps

<div class="cards three">
  <div><h5>Why Checkout Rate Increased More Than Add-to-Cart Rate</h5><p>After the homepage redesign, cart additions rose by 26%, but checkouts jumped 49%, suggesting the new layout attracted higher-intent users and reduced friction in the funnel.</p></div>
  <div><h5>Validate Assumptions: Buyer Intent &amp; Drop-Off Insights</h5><p>Despite the improvement, checkout rates still lag behind industry benchmarks. I’ll review session replays and refine questions for upcoming interviews to better understand cart-to-checkout drop-off behavior.</p></div>
  <div><h5>Next Focus: Shopping Cart Redesign to further improve conversion rate</h5><p>Clarify pricing, shipping, and discount visibility in the cart, conduct internal usability testing, and streamline the mobile checkout flow for better conversion.</p></div>
</div>

## Reflection

This project taught me how to **align design impact with business goals**. By framing changes in terms of **conversion uplift and engagement metrics**, I gained buy-in and support from both product and marketing.
