## What's Shepherd Money?

### An ai-powered personalized financial planning platform

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/LbjKhDOubfwbLy2YpinZQuSP8UA.png?width=2946&height=1428)

Shepherd Money helps users manage every aspect of their finances—from budgeting and net worth tracking to personalized investment strategies—all with a focus on tailored insights and ease of use.

## Research Insights

I conducted competitive analysis and user interviews to uncover the pain points in the old budgeting feature.

<div class="cards three">
  <div><h5>Poor Navigation Structure</h5><p>Unclear interface makes budgeting features hard to find, causing frustration and inefficient finance tracking.</p></div>
  <div><h5>Ineffective Feature Exposure</h5><p>Poor feature visibility leads to missed opportunities for better financial management.</p></div>
  <div><h5>Rigid Budgeting Workflows</h5><p>Limited budget customization restricts users from managing finances according to their goals.</p></div>
</div>

![The old website product](https://framerusercontent.com/images/HKFnGZT1CyNiGQrvPI8yUWac.png?width=1920&height=1005)

<p class="caption">The old website product</p>

### User interviews indicate that users need personalized features to address unique financial goals.

We conducted **interviews with 5 users** from different occupations to understand their financial needs. Early-career users face distinct challenges, such as student loans and limited financial knowledge, setting them apart from general users. Therefore, their short-term financial goals require a more **tailored budgeting experience.**

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/xWwwrL9QqVHJzvkWxTuXGeGnhY.png?width=10758&height=7167)

### Competitor analysis reveals best practices for dashboard-centric and card-based design patterns.

Across various personal finance apps, we found that **dashboard-centric design** enhances engagement by providing a clear overview of budgets, spending trends, and goals. A **card-based UI** improves usability, allowing users to track and adjust budgets effortlessly.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/6VOyigyE4QtZmQBp9YPqSg4XwM.png?width=640&height=470)

## Reflection

### Things I would do different next time

<div class="cards three">
  <div><h5>Effective Communication with Pixels and Color Codes</h5><p>When collaborating on the project, one valuable lesson is the importance of using specific units like pixels (px) and color codes to communicate design elements accurately. Additionally, specifying color codes precisely, such as hex codes or RGB values, helps maintain color consistency throughout the project.</p></div>
  <div><h5>Prioritizing Functionality before Visual Design</h5><p>By doing this, I established a strong foundation for the design system, ensuring that it meets user needs and functional requirements. Once the functionality is well-defined, I could then layer visual design elements on top, creating a visually appealing and consistent user interface that aligns with the system's functional components.</p></div>
  <div><h5>Explore more solutions before diving into one</h5><p>Due to time limitations, we didn’t explore many solution options. Next time, I would test each solution more thoroughly before selecting the one that best fits the problem statement and business goals.</p></div>
</div>
